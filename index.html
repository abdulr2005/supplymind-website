01 / The problem

One supply chain.
Too many disconnected conversations.

Inventory, purchasing, suppliers and logistics are closely linked. When their information and decisions sit apart, it becomes harder to see the whole picture.

01

Information in fragments

Operational context can be scattered across records, teams and day-to-day conversations.

02

Coordination by hand

Connecting the dots often depends on people carrying context from one workflow to another.

03

A picture that is hard to read

Decision-makers need a clear summary of what matters across the operation.

02 / The SupplyMind AI approach

Specialized perspectives.
Shared coordination.

The original prototype uses a Master Agent to coordinate specialized capabilities across the supply chain.

The concept brings inventory, procurement, supplier management, logistics, analysis and executive reporting into a coordinated structure. Its focus is how these areas work together, rather than treating each decision in isolation.

06 / Current Stage & Vision

A working idea.
An open exploration.

Prototype stage

SupplyMind AI is a prototype-stage multi-agent supply chain management concept.

The founder is exploring product validation, real business needs and potential commercialization. The vision is a more connected approach to supply chain decision support, shaped by learning and practical feedback.

Mentorship can help sharpen the questions: where is coordination most difficult, what do businesses actually need, and where could this concept be useful?

This showcase describes the current concept. It carries no fixed timelines or delivery commitments. The original multi-agent system’s source code is not publicly available.
